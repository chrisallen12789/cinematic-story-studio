# Point-of-view model

Phase 2 represents first person, second person, third-person limited,
third-person omniscient, mixed, experimental, and unknown narration. A segment
may identify an evidence-backed viewpoint or narrator character and a shift at
a scene boundary, within a scene, or at an uncertain boundary.

POV is an interpretive machine proposal. Character-name frequency alone cannot
select a viewpoint character. Each segment records its exact range, evidence,
confidence, warnings, producer, and effective authority. Conflicting modes or
viewpoint evidence remain explicit.

Human correction can change or clear a mode/viewpoint and lock the effective
result through the common immutable overlay. The original proposal remains
inspectable, and changed POV evidence invalidates only gates whose enumerated
fingerprint includes that segment.
