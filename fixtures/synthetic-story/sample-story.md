# Chapter One: The Quiet Relay

## Scene One: Platform Glass

Captain Mira Vale crossed the Relay Platform while rain traced silver paths down
the roof. Tovin Rook, whom she still called Mr. Rook when she was angry, held a
brass lantern over a faded map. Engineer Jun Pell waited by the signal cabinet.
Mira wished Tovin would admit why the northern marker frightened him.

"The northern marker is awake again," Tovin said.

Mira set down her case. "Then it remembered us before we remembered it."

A brass plaque read "KEEP THE LANTERN LIT." The words were an instruction, not
a voice. Not again, Mira thought.

"We can still turn back."

No one claimed the warning. Mira and Tovin each watched the other.

---

## Scene Two: The Clock Room

Three minutes later, Mira led Tovin and Jun into the Clock Room. Captain Nessa
Quill, also known as Archivist Quill, stood beside Inspector Ilya Maren. Keeper
Orin Dax guarded the eastern door. Dr. Elian Sorell and Dr. Sana Vey compared
two clocks whose hands moved backward.

"Only one of us remembers the first signal."

Neither captain moved. The title could mean Mira or Nessa, and everyone heard
the uncertainty.

Alex Reed checked the west dial while another Alex Reed checked the east dial.
"The west dial is clear," Alex said.
The record did not establish which Alex had spoken.

Mira accused Tovin of hiding the old route. Their strained partnership became
open distrust. Tovin wanted to explain, but fear kept him silent.

The brass lantern slipped from Jun's hand. Its glass shattered on the stone.
Mira left the broken frame beside the backward clock.

# Chapter Two: Borrowed Hours

## Scene Three: Six Years Earlier, Archive Vault

Six years earlier, Tovin had followed Nessa into the Archive Vault beneath the
relay. Back then, Archivist Quill was his rival, not his ally. Inspector Maren
had not yet earned her badge, and young Jun called Orin "Keeper Dax."

"Hide the route until Mira is ready," Nessa told Tovin.

Dr. Sorell sealed the map case while Doctor Vey recorded the time as nine
minutes after midnight. Tovin remembered choosing secrecy to protect Mira, and
he hated the choice even before it was made.

---

## Scene Four: North Signal Tower

Back in the present, two hours before dawn, Tovin climbed the North Signal
Tower. For the first time that night, only he noticed how every bell matched his
heartbeat. He feared Mira would never trust him again.

The same brass lantern hung unbroken from the tower hook, its glass clean and
its flame steady. No one explained how it had left the Clock Room.

Mira reached the landing and lowered her voice. "I was wrong to call you a
liar."

"You were right to ask," Tovin said.

Their distrust eased into a cautious alliance. Below them, Nessa opened the
sealed route for the first time in six years.

---

# Chapter Three: The Last Signal

## Scene Five: Flooded Concourse

At the Flooded Concourse, anger carried Mira through the black water. When Orin
admitted that her missing brother had sent the first signal, anger gave way to
grief. Jun caught her arm, and Nessa abandoned their old rivalry to help.

Elian and Sana stood together at the control rail.

"Close the red valve," the doctor said.

Both doctors reached for it. The speaker remained unresolved.

Mira steadied herself. Her grief became resolve: she intended to answer the
signal and bring everyone home.

---

## Scene Six: Dawn Switchyard

At dawn, the eight travelers gathered in the Dawn Switchyard: Mira, Tovin, Jun,
Nessa, Ilya, Orin, Elian, and Sana. Inspector Maren locked the western gate
while Engineer Pell reset the relay.

"Tomorrow, the northern line will carry passengers again," Mira said.

Nessa offered Mira the archive key. What began as rivalry ended as trust. Tovin
raised the lantern, and this time Mira did not look away.
