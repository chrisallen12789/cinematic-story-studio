# Chapter One: The Quiet Relay

## Scene One: Platform Glass

Mira Vale crossed the empty relay platform while rain traced silver paths down
the glass roof. At the signal cabinet, Tovin Rook held a brass lantern over a
map whose ink had faded to gray.

"The northern marker is awake again," Tovin said.

Mira set her travel case beside the map. "Then it remembered us before we
remembered it."

---

## Scene Two: The Unmarked Door

Behind the cabinet, a narrow door stood open where the wall had been solid that
morning. Mira lifted the lantern, and Tovin listened to the soft ticking beyond
the threshold.

"We can still turn back."

Neither traveler claimed the warning. The ticking paused, as if the hidden room
were waiting for one of them to answer.

# Chapter Two: A Borrowed Dawn

## Scene Three: The Clock Room

Rows of sleeping clocks filled the chamber. Tovin touched none of them. Mira
found one small dial moving backward and covered it with her hand.

"I will keep the door in sight," Mira said.

Tovin nodded. "And I will find what woke the marker."
